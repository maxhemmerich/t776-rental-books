When is Form T776 due?

Form T776 is the statement of rental income and expenses that is filed with your T1 return, so its deadline is your T1 deadline. This page prints the Canada Revenue Agency's own published filing and payment dates - the wording is the CRA's, quoted from the page named under it, with the date it was read - and nothing here is our wording.

The ordinary filing and payment date

“For most people, the 2025 return has to be filed on or before April 30, 2026, and payment is due April 30, 2026.”

Read from Canada Revenue Agency, Filing due dates for the 2025 tax return on 2026-10-09.

The later filing date for those who carried on a business

“your 2025 return must be filed by June 15, 2026”

Read from Canada Revenue Agency, Filing due dates for the 2025 tax return on 2026-10-09.

This is a filing date, not a payment date

“However, if you have a balance owing for 2025, you must pay it on or before April 30, 2026.”

Read from Canada Revenue Agency, Filing due dates for the 2025 tax return on 2026-10-09.

When the deadline is a weekend or a public holiday

“When a due date falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or public holiday recognized by the CRA, your return is considered on time if the CRA receives it, or if it is postmarked, on or before the next business day.”

Read from Canada Revenue Agency, Filing due dates for the 2025 tax return on 2026-10-09.

The same two dates, as CRA lists them

“April 30, 2026: Deadline to file your taxes”

Read from Canada Revenue Agency, Due dates and payment dates - Personal income tax on 2026-10-09.

The later date CRA lists for the self-employed

“June 15, 2026: Deadline to file your taxes if you or your spouse or common-law partner are self-employed”

Read from Canada Revenue Agency, Due dates and payment dates - Personal income tax on 2026-10-09.

The payment date CRA lists

“April 30, 2026: Deadline to pay your taxes”

Read from Canada Revenue Agency, Due dates and payment dates - Personal income tax on 2026-10-09.

CRA's own one-line summary of the two filing dates

“Filing due date is April 30 for most taxpayers, June 15 for self-employed, and may vary for a deceased person’s return”

Read from Canada Revenue Agency, Due dates and payment dates - Personal income tax on 2026-10-09.

If your situation is not the ordinary one, or you are not sure which date applies to you, that is the moment to ask the CRA or a qualified accountant - before the deadline, not after.
