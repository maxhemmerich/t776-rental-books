# T776 Rental Books

A bookkeeping workbook for Canadian landlords who file **Form T776, Statement of Real Estate
Rentals** with their T1 personal return, and who currently track rent and expenses by hand in a
spreadsheet they built themselves.

This repository holds the public page and the free reference guide.

- Live page: https://maxhemmerich.github.io/t776-rental-books/
- Free guide: [T776-Field-Guide.pdf](T776-Field-Guide.pdf)

## The paid product

**T776 Rental Books**, $29 CAD one-time, is a Microsoft Excel workbook with eight tabs:

`START HERE` · `Setup` · `Rent Ledger` · `Expenses` · `Mileage` · `T776 Summary` · `Capital & CCA` · `T776 Codes`

You log rent as you receive it and expenses as you pay them. The T776 Summary tab totals gross
rents and every expense line per property and per CRA line number, so the figures can be copied
onto Form T776 or handed to an accountant. Expenses are flagged Repair or Capital; capital rows
are kept out of the current-year summary and listed separately.

The workbook is not in this repository — it is the paid file. It is delivered after purchase.

## What the workbook does not do

It does not file your taxes and it does not calculate capital cost allowance. It is built for
long-term residential rentals, not short-term or Airbnb lets. It does not connect to a bank.

## Disclaimer

T776 Rental Books is a bookkeeping aid. It is not tax, legal, or accounting advice. Check your
categories and allocations with a qualified accountant.

Expense line numbers and names in the guide and the workbook follow the Canada Revenue Agency's
published Form T776.
