The chore this replaces

You track rent and expenses in a spreadsheet you built yourself. It holds up during the year, and then the year ends and you spend a weekend folding it into the lines Form T776 asks for. This workbook keeps those lines from the first entry onward, so the summary is finished before you file.

What it does not do

  • It does not file your taxes. You enter the totals on Form T776 yourself.
  • It does not calculate capital cost allowance. It lists your capital items so your accountant can.
  • It is built for long-term residential rentals. Short-term and Airbnb books do not fit it.
  • It does not connect to your bank. You type the numbers in.
