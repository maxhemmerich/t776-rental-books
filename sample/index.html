The formula that does the totalling

The T776 Summary is not typed in. It reads the Rent Ledger and the Expenses tab and totals them per property and per CRA line number. These are the file's own formulas, quoted from the cells:

The gross-rents row sums the Rent Ledger by property and by the tax year in the one control cell. As it reads in the file:

=SUMIFS('Rent Ledger'!$G$5:$G$12,'Rent Ledger'!$B$5:$B$12,Setup!$A$5,'Rent Ledger'!$A$5:$A$12,">="&DATE($B$2,1,1),'Rent Ledger'!$A$5:$A$12,"<="&DATE($B$2,12,31))

Each expense line is a SUMPRODUCT of the Expenses tab: it matches the line code, the property and the tax year, keeps Capital rows out, and multiplies each amount by that row's rental share. As it reads in the file:

=SUMPRODUCT((LEFT(Expenses!$C$5:$C$12,4)=$A15)*(Expenses!$B$5:$B$12=Setup!$A$5)*(Expenses!$I$5:$I$12<>"Capital")*(Expenses!$A$5:$A$12>=DATE($B$2,1,1))*(Expenses!$A$5:$A$12<=DATE($B$2,12,31))*Expenses!$F$5:$F$12*Expenses!$K$5:$K$12)

And the net line is the one subtraction the whole book builds to:

=E5-E21
