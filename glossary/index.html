10 Terms used in this guide

Short, plain definitions of the words this guide uses. They describe what each term means on the form, not how it applies to your own situation.

Term What it means here
CRA the Canada Revenue Agency, the federal body that publishes Form T776 and the guidance referred to throughout this guide.
Form T776 / Statement of Real Estate Rentals the schedule filed with a T1 personal income tax return by an individual who owns rental property in their own name.
T1 the personal income tax return that Form T776 is filed with.
Gross rents (8141) the total rent collected for the year, before any expenses.
Amount A total deductible expenses, after the personal portion of shared costs has been removed.
Personal portion (9949) the share of shared costs that relates to your own use of the property. It is reported on the form and is not deducted.
Net income (loss) before adjustments (9369) gross rental income minus amount A. This is the figure carried to your T1.
Current expense a cost that restores the property to its previous condition. Deducted in the year it is incurred - for repairs, on line 8960.
Capital expense a cost that improves the property beyond its previous condition. Added to the cost of the property rather than deducted in one year.
CCA (capital cost allowance) the write-off of a capital cost over time. The property classes and the rates are set by CRA.
Mileage log the record of date, destination, purpose and kilometres that supports a motor vehicle claim on line 9281.

If a definition here does not fit your situation, that is the moment to ask, before you file - not after.
